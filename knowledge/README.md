# Sample knowledge library (placeholders)

These files exist so Ben runs end-to-end out of the box. They are **short paraphrased
summaries written for testing, not authoritative text**. Replace them with your real
documents (official regulation text, standards you are licensed to use, internal policies,
and your AI Risk & Control Library) and re-run `ben ingest ./knowledge`.

| File | Format | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sample_eu_ai_act_summary.md` | Markdown | Summary of selected EU AI Act articles |
| `sample_iso42001_overview.docx` | DOCX | Overview of ISO/IEC 42001 clauses |
| `sample_uae_pdpl_notes.pdf` | PDF | Internal guidance notes on UAE PDPL and AI |
| `ai_risk_control_library.xlsx` | XLSX | 10-row sample AI Risk & Control Library |

The DOCX, PDF and XLSX are generated by `scripts/make_sample_knowledge.py`.
This README is skipped by ingestion.
